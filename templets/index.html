About This Project

PhishAware is a cybersecurity awareness project designed to help users recognize phishing attacks and protect themselves from online threats.

🎯 Project Goal

To raise awareness about phishing attacks and teach users how to identify suspicious messages and links.

📚 What You Can Learn

Explore security tips, phishing examples, educational link analysis, and an interactive quiz.

💻 Technologies Used

Python, Flask, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript were used to build this project.

Note: The link analyzer uses educational examples only. It does not scan or verify real URLs.

Security Tips

Simple habits to keep yourself safe online.

📧

Check the Sender

Always check the sender's email address before opening messages.

🔗

Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click on unknown or suspicious links.

🔒

Never Share Passwords

Never share your passwords or personal information with unknown sources.

Common Phishing Signs

Urgent Messages

Messages that ask you to act immediately.

Suspicious Links

Links that lead to unknown or unusual websites.

Personal Information Requests

Emails asking for your password or bank details.

Phishing Examples

Learn how to recognize suspicious messages.

📧 SUSPICIOUS MESSAGE

Fake Bank Message

Your bank account is locked!

Verify your details immediately.

⚠ Warning Signs

Urgent requests for personal information.

🎁 SUSPICIOUS MESSAGE

Fake Prize Message

Congratulations! You have won a free gift.

Send your personal details to claim it.

⚠ Warning Signs

Unexpected prizes and requests for personal data.

Question 2: Suspicious Email

Question 3: Suspicious Attachment

You receive an email from an unknown sender with an attachment named Free_Gift.exe. What should you do?

Question 4: Fake Link

You receive an email asking you to verify your bank account. The link looks similar to your bank's official website but has a slightly different address. What should you do?

Question 5: Password Theft

You receive an email asking you to enter your password to prevent your account from being closed. What should you do?

Question 6: Fake Prize

You receive a message saying you won a free smartphone, but you must pay a small fee to receive your prize. What should you do?

Question 7: Fake Bank Alert

You receive a text message claiming to be from your bank. It says your account will be blocked unless you verify your details immediately. What should you do?

Question 8: Fake Company Message

You receive an email from a company claiming you have won a special offer. The sender's email address looks suspicious. What should you do?

Question 9: Personal Information Theft

You receive a message from an unknown sender asking for your full name, address, and ID number to receive a free gift. What should you do?

Question 10: Money Transfer Scam
